# Rapid Response Team Activation Criteria

## Purpose
The Rapid Response Team (RRT) brings critical care expertise to the bedside before a patient deteriorates further.

## Activation criteria
Call the rapid response team for any of the following: systolic blood pressure below 90 mmHg, heart rate above 130 or below 40 beats per minute, respiratory rate above 28 or below 8 breaths per minute, oxygen saturation below 90% despite oxygen, acute change in level of consciousness, or any staff member worried about the patient.

## How to call
Dial extension 4444 and state "Rapid Response" with the unit and room number.

## After the call
The primary nurse stays with the patient and gives a brief SBAR handoff to the team. Document the event in the record.
