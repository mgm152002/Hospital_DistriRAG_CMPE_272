# High-Alert Medication Independent Double-Check

## Medications that need a double-check
Insulin infusions, heparin infusions, opioid patient-controlled analgesia (PCA) and chemotherapy require an independent double-check by a second licensed clinician before starting and at every bag or rate change.

## How to double-check
The second clinician checks the order, the drug, the concentration, the pump settings and the patient identifiers independently, without being told the expected answer.

## Weight-based dosing
Do not calculate weight-based doses at the bedside. Request a pharmacy consult for weight-based dosing questions.
