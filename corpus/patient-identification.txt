Patient Identification: Two Identifiers

1. Purpose
Correct identification prevents wrong-patient errors.

2. Procedure
Use two identifiers, the patient's full name and date of birth, before giving medications, blood products, collecting specimens or performing procedures.
Compare both identifiers against the wristband and the order.
The room or bed number is never an identifier.

3. When identifiers do not match
Stop. Do not proceed until the mismatch is resolved with the charge nurse.
