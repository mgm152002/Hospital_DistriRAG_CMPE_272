# Contact and Droplet Isolation Precautions

## Contact precautions
Use contact precautions for MRSA, VRE, C. difficile and multidrug-resistant organisms. Wear a gown and gloves on entering the room and remove them before leaving.

## C. difficile
Use soap and water for hand hygiene, not alcohol rub alone, and clean the room with a sporicidal agent at discharge.

## Droplet precautions
Use droplet precautions for influenza and pertussis. Wear a surgical mask within 2 metres (6 feet) of the patient.

## Signage and transport
Place the isolation sign on the door. Flag isolation status on every patient transport request.

## Ending precautions
Infection Prevention decides when precautions can be discontinued.
