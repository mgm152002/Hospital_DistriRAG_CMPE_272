# Pressure Injury Prevention

## Risk assessment
Complete the Braden Scale on admission and daily. A Braden score of 18 or lower means the patient is at risk.

## Prevention
Reposition at-risk patients at least every 2 hours, float heels, use a pressure-redistributing mattress, and keep skin clean and dry.

## Documentation
Inspect skin every shift and document any reddened area. Refer stage 2 or deeper injuries to the wound care nurse within 24 hours.
