# Discharge Instructions: Heart Failure

## Daily weights
Weigh yourself every morning after using the toilet and before breakfast.

## When to call
Call your care team if your weight goes up by more than 1 kg in a day or 2 kg in a week, if you are more short of breath, or if your ankles swell.

## Diet
Limit salt and follow the fluid limit your team gave you.

## Medications
Take your medicines exactly as listed on your discharge medication list.
