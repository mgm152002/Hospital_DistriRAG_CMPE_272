# Central Line Insertion and Maintenance Bundle (CLABSI Prevention)

## Purpose
Reduce central line-associated bloodstream infections (CLABSI) to zero by applying the same evidence-based bundle at
every insertion and every day the line stays in.

## Insertion bundle
Perform hand hygiene immediately before insertion. Use maximal sterile barrier precautions: cap, mask, sterile gown,
sterile gloves and a full-body sterile drape. Prepare the skin with chlorhexidine-alcohol and let it dry completely
(at least 30 seconds). Prefer the subclavian site for adults when clinically appropriate; avoid the femoral site.
An observer completes the insertion checklist and may stop the procedure if a step is missed.

## Daily maintenance
Review the need for the line every day on rounds and remove it as soon as it is no longer needed. Scrub the hub for
15 seconds with an antiseptic before every access. Change transparent dressings every 7 days, or sooner if damp,
loose or soiled; change gauze dressings every 2 days. Use chlorhexidine-impregnated sponges at the insertion site.

## Documentation and audit
Document insertion date, site, operator and checklist completion. Infection Prevention audits bundle compliance
monthly and reports unit results to the Quality Committee.
