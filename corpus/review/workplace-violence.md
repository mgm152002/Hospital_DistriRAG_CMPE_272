# Workplace Violence Prevention and Response

## Purpose
Protect staff, patients and visitors from verbal and physical aggression, and make sure every incident is reported
and followed up.

## Recognising escalation
Early signs include raised voice, pacing, clenched fists, threats, and refusal to follow reasonable requests. Staff
should keep a clear exit path, avoid standing between the person and the door, and speak calmly in short sentences.

## Calling for help
Dial extension 4444 and say "Code Grey" with the unit name for a person who is aggressive but unarmed. For a person
with a weapon, dial 4444 and say "Code Silver"; do not attempt to disarm them. Security responds to both codes.

## After an incident
Make sure anyone injured is assessed. Report the incident in the safety reporting system before the end of the shift.
The unit manager holds a debrief within 72 hours and offers staff referral to the Employee Assistance Program.

## Training
All patient-facing staff complete de-escalation training at hire and every year after that.
