# Sharps Injury and Bloodborne Exposure

## Immediately after an exposure
Wash the area with soap and water. Flush splashes to the eyes or mouth with water for 15 minutes.

## Reporting
Report the exposure to Occupational Health within 1 hour, any time of day, by calling extension 3030.

## Prevention
Never recap needles. Dispose of sharps in a sharps container at the point of use.
