# Anticoagulation Reversal Reference

## When to consider reversal
Consider reversal for life-threatening bleeding or an emergency procedure in a patient taking an anticoagulant.

## Process
Contact the on-call clinical pharmacist and the treating physician. Dosing of reversal agents follows the pharmacy anticoagulation protocol; do not dose from this reference.

## Monitoring
Repeat coagulation tests after reversal as directed by the protocol and document the time of each dose.
