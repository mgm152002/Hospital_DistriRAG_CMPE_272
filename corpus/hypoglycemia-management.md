# Hypoglycemia Management Protocol

## Definition
Hypoglycemia is a blood glucose below 70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L).

## Treatment for a patient who can swallow
Give 15 grams of fast-acting carbohydrate and recheck glucose in 15 minutes. Repeat until glucose is 70 mg/dL or higher.

## Patient who cannot swallow
Follow the emergency hypoglycemia order set and notify the provider.
