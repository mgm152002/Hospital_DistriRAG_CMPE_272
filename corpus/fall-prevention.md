# Fall Risk Assessment and Prevention

## Assessment
Assess fall risk with the Morse Fall Scale on admission, every shift, after a fall, and after any change in condition.

## Risk levels
A Morse score of 45 or higher means high fall risk. A score of 25 to 44 means moderate risk.

## Prevention bundle
Keep the bed in the lowest position with brakes locked, keep the call light within reach, use non-slip socks, and complete hourly rounding for high-risk patients.

## After a fall
Assess the patient for injury before moving them, notify the provider, complete a post-fall huddle within the shift, and document the fall in the incident reporting system.
