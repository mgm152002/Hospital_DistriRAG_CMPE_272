Blood Transfusion Administration

1. Before the transfusion
Confirm consent, check two patient identifiers with a second clinician, and record baseline vital signs.

2. During the transfusion
Record vital signs 15 minutes after starting. Stay with the patient for the first 15 minutes.

3. Reactions
Stop the transfusion immediately for fever, rash, breathing difficulty or low blood pressure, and call the provider.
