Controlled Substance Waste and Reconciliation

1. Witnessed waste
Every controlled substance waste needs a second licensed witness who watches the waste and co-signs the record.

2. Timing
Document waste before the end of the shift.

3. Discrepancies
Report any count discrepancy to pharmacy within 24 hours. Pharmacy reconciles dispensing cabinet records daily.
