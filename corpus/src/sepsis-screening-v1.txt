Sepsis Screening and Escalation Protocol

1. Purpose
Early recognition of sepsis saves lives.

2. Screening criteria
Screen for sepsis when two or more are present: temperature above 38.3 C or below 36 C, heart rate above 90, respiratory rate above 20, or new confusion.

3. Escalation
Notify the provider within 15 minutes of a positive screen and draw a lactate level.
