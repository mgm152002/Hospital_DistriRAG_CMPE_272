Restraint and Seclusion Policy

1. Least restrictive first
Use restraint only when less restrictive measures have failed.

2. Orders
Obtain a provider order within 1 hour of starting restraint. Adult orders must be renewed every 4 hours.

3. Monitoring
Monitor the restrained patient at least every 15 minutes and document circulation, skin and comfort.
