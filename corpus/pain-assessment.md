# Pain Assessment and Reassessment

## Assessment
Assess pain on admission, every shift and with each new report of pain, using the 0 to 10 numeric scale or a behavioural scale for patients who cannot self-report.

## Reassessment
Reassess pain within 30 minutes after an intravenous opioid and within 60 minutes after an oral analgesic.

## Sedation
Check sedation with the Pasero Opioid-Induced Sedation Scale whenever opioids are given.
