# Stroke Alert Protocol

## Recognising stroke
Use BE-FAST: balance, eyes, face, arm, speech and time.

## Last known well
Establish the time the patient was last known well. Activate a stroke alert when new stroke symptoms began within the last 24 hours.

## Activation
Dial extension 4455 and state "Stroke Alert". Check a bedside glucose immediately, because low glucose can mimic stroke.
