# Hand Hygiene Policy

## Purpose
Clean hands are the single most effective way to prevent healthcare-associated infection at Northbridge Synthetic Hospital.

## Scope
All staff, students, contractors and volunteers who enter patient care areas.

## Moments for hand hygiene
Perform hand hygiene before touching a patient, before a clean or aseptic procedure, after body fluid exposure risk, after touching a patient, and after touching patient surroundings.

## Alcohol-based hand rub
Rub all surfaces of both hands with alcohol-based hand rub for 20 to 30 seconds until dry. This replaces the 15-second guidance in version 1.

## Soap and water
Use soap and water when hands are visibly soiled and when caring for patients with suspected or confirmed C. difficile. Wash for 40 to 60 seconds.

## Gloves
Gloves do not replace hand hygiene. Perform hand hygiene before putting on and after removing gloves.

## Audits
Infection Prevention observes 50 hand hygiene opportunities per unit each month and reports compliance to unit managers.
