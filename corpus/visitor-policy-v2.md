# Visitor Policy

## Visiting hours
General visiting hours are 7:00 to 21:00 every day. This replaces the 8:00 to 20:00 hours in version 1.

## Visitors at the bedside
Two visitors at a time may stay at the bedside.

## Restrictions
Visitors with fever, cough or vomiting should not visit. Isolation rooms require visitors to follow the posted precautions.

## Overnight stays
One support person may stay overnight with approval from the charge nurse.
