Lab Critical Value Notification

1. What counts as critical
A critical value is a laboratory result that may need urgent action, as listed in the laboratory critical value table.

2. Notification
The laboratory calls the responsible nurse or provider. The receiver writes down and reads back the result.

3. Timing
The provider must be notified within 30 minutes of the laboratory call. Document the time and the person notified.
