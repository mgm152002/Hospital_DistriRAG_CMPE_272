# Chest Pain Pathway

## First ten minutes
Obtain a 12-lead ECG within 10 minutes of arrival for any patient with chest pain or anginal equivalent.

## Escalation
Show the ECG to the emergency physician immediately. Activate the cardiac catheterisation team per the STEMI protocol when the physician confirms ST-elevation.

## Monitoring
Place the patient on continuous cardiac monitoring and repeat the ECG if symptoms change.
