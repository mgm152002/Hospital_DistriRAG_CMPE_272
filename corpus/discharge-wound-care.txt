Discharge Instructions: Post-op Wound Care

1. Keeping the wound clean
Keep the dressing dry for 48 hours. After 48 hours you may shower and pat the wound dry.

2. Signs of infection
Call your surgeon if you notice increasing redness, warmth, swelling, pus, or a temperature above 38 C.

3. Follow-up
Keep your follow-up appointment for wound review and stitch or staple removal.
