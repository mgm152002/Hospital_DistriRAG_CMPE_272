# Contrast Allergy Premedication Reference

## Identifying risk
Ask every patient about previous reactions to iodinated contrast before scheduling a contrast study.

## Process
For a prior moderate or severe reaction, the radiologist decides whether contrast is needed and whether the radiology premedication protocol applies. Medication choice and timing follow that protocol.

## Documentation
Record the prior reaction and the plan in the imaging request.
